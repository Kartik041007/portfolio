Cooked over real fire, on purpose

PARAM & FOODS started as a single tandoor on a rented rooftop in 2014. Eleven years on, the kitchen still runs on coal, clay, and a short list of ingredients bought the same morning they're cooked.

No microwaves, no shortcuts through a fryer basket. If a dish can't be made properly over fire, it doesn't go on the menu.
